14th National SIF Meet

#MENtality2024 — Chandigarh, August 15-17, 2024

About the Event

The 14th National Save Indian Family Meet brought together over 170 participants from across India for three days of discussions, workshops, and solidarity building around men's rights and family welfare issues.

Held in Chandigarh from August 15-17, 2024, under the theme #MENtality2024, the event featured keynote speeches from legal experts, activists, and affected individuals who shared their stories and strategies for change. Participants included representatives from men's rights organizations across 15+ states, lawyers specializing in family law, counsellors, and families who have benefited from SIF's support network.

Key topics discussed included legislative reforms needed for gender-neutral laws, mental health support for falsely accused men, custody battle strategies, and building a stronger national network for advocacy and mutual aid.

Key Highlights

Legislative Reform Proposals

Detailed presentations on proposed amendments to IPC 498A, DV Act, and Hindu Marriage Act were discussed, with consensus on pushing for mandatory preliminary inquiry before arrests.

Mental Health Initiative

Launch of a nationwide counselling network connecting affected men with volunteer therapists and peer support groups in their cities.

Legal Aid Expansion

Announcement of expanded pro-bono legal aid programs in 10 new cities, with empanelled lawyers trained in men's rights cases.

Youth Engagement

New campus ambassador program launched to engage college students in awareness activities about gender-neutral laws and family values.
