Aims & Objectives

Our Mission

Pati Pariwar Kalyan Samiti is dedicated to protecting family values, supporting men facing false allegations, and promoting gender-neutral laws that ensure justice for all members of the Indian family unit.

We believe in strengthening families through dialogue, legal awareness, counselling, and community support. Our organization works tirelessly to create a balanced society where both men and women can live with dignity, respect, and equal protection under the law.

Our Objectives

⚖️

Legal Support

Provide free legal aid and guidance to men facing false domestic violence cases, dowry harassment, and custody battles.

💬

Counselling Services

Offer professional counselling for families in distress, helping couples resolve conflicts through dialogue rather than litigation.

📢

Awareness Campaigns

Conduct public awareness programs about misuse of gender-biased laws and promote gender-neutral legislation.

🤝

Family Reconciliation

Work towards reuniting separated families through mediation and fostering understanding between spouses.

📚

Education & Research

Publish research papers, organize seminars, and educate the public about family welfare and men's issues.

🏛️

Policy Advocacy

Engage with lawmakers and government bodies to advocate for balanced, gender-neutral family laws.

Who We Help

Falsely Accused Men

Men facing false allegations of domestic violence, dowry harassment (IPC 498A), and other criminal charges filed during marital disputes.

Fathers Seeking Custody

Fathers fighting for equal parenting rights and child custody in a legal system that often favors mothers without considering the father's role.

Elderly Parents

Senior citizens abandoned or mistreated by their children, seeking maintenance and respect under the Maintenance of Parents Act.

Domestic Violence Victims

Men who are victims of domestic abuse but have no legal recourse due to gender-specific violence laws.

Financial Exploitation Survivors

Individuals facing financial extortion through misuse of maintenance laws and false monetary claims.

Families in Crisis

Entire families going through separation, divorce proceedings, or severe marital discord needing mediation support.
