Weekly Meetings

Regular Support Group Meetings

Day: Every Sunday

Time: 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Location: 9/215, Sector 9, Jankipuram, Lucknow

Format: Open discussion, case reviews, legal guidance, peer support

All members and those seeking help are welcome to attend.

Confirm Attendance

Our weekly meetings provide a safe space for members to share experiences, seek advice, and receive support from fellow members who understand their struggles. Legal experts and counsellors often attend to provide professional guidance.

Public Outreach

Connecting with the Community

Pati Pariwar Kalyan Samiti believes in reaching out to the public through various channels to spread awareness about family welfare, legal rights, and the importance of gender-neutral laws.

Our outreach programs include street plays, pamphlet distribution, community meetings, school and college seminars, and collaboration with other social organizations working towards similar goals.

How to Connect With Us:

  • Visit our office during working hours (Mon-Sat, 10 AM - 6 PM)
  • Call our helpline for immediate assistance
  • Attend our weekly Sunday meetings
  • Follow us on social media for updates
  • Send us an email with your queries
